Cookie Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: 11 SEPTEMBER 2026 | ENTITY: CNEI

1. Zero-Cookie Architecture

The CNEI web architecture (cnei.org.uk) maintains a strict zero-cookie and zero-tracking standard. We do not set, store, or execute:

  • First-party or third-party cookies.
  • Tracking pixels or web beacons.
  • HTML5 Local Storage, Session Storage, or IndexedDB objects.
  • Third-party web analytics frameworks (e.g., Google Analytics).

2. Statutory Exemption (PECR 2003)

Because this platform stores no information on—and retrieves no information from—your terminal equipment, this website does not require or display a cookie consent banner under Regulation 6 of the Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003 (PECR) or the UK GDPR.

3. Local Resource Isolation

All page styles and metadata are served locally from our primary repository. No external resources, Content Delivery Networks (CDNs), or third-party font calls (such as Google Fonts) are invoked during your site session.

Information Provenance & Operational Notice All analytical outputs and materials published by CNEI are derived strictly from publicly available, unclassified, and lawfully accessible open sources. CNEI does not handle, store, or process classified government documents, leaked materials, or non-consensual personal data. CNEI is an independent, unincorporated, voluntary research collective operated by student researchers across the United Kingdom and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government body, military authority, or official intelligence agency.
