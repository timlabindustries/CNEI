Analytical Standards

Research Methodology

The 4-phase structured verification framework applied to all intelligence briefs published by CNEI.

PHASE 01 // DATA COLLECTION

Public Identification & Ingestion

CNEI ingests data exclusively from lawful, unclassified open sources. Primary streams include commercial satellite imagery, maritime AIS telemetry, ADS-B flight-tracking data, and official public regulatory filings.

PHASE 02 // VERIFICATION

Telemetry & Geospatial Cross-Referencing

No single data point is accepted as factual truth. Every data stream undergoes multi-angle cross-verification, matching satellite imagery against RF signals, flight paths, and geographical terrain features.

PHASE 03 // AUDITING

Noise Reduction & Bias Elimination

Analytical outputs must remain strictly objective and free from editorial speculation. Researchers scrub reports of subjective commentary, ensuring conclusions rest solely on empirical data points.

PHASE 04 // PUBLICATION

Transparent Citation & Confidence Scoring

Every published intelligence brief includes clear source references and confidence markers, enabling readers to independently audit and verify our underlying datasets line by line.

Information Provenance & Operational Notice All analytical outputs published by CNEI are derived exclusively from publicly accessible, lawful open sources. CNEI does not handle or process classified government documents or non-consensual personal data. CNEI is an independent, un-incorporated voluntary student-led research collective and is not affiliated with any official government body or intelligence agency.
