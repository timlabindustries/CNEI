About CNEI

The Center for National & External Intelligence is an independent, voluntary research collective focused on providing rigorous open-source intelligence (OSINT) and strategic analysis on geostrategic affairs and national security.

Our work leverages publicly available satellite imagery, maritime AIS data, flight telemetry, and structured verification methods to deliver clear, unbiased reporting.
