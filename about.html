ABOUT CNEI & OPERATIONAL CODE

Structure, Methodology, and Legal Boundaries

Who We Are

The Centre of National and External Intelligence (CNEI) is an unincorporated, non-monetary, student-led open-source intelligence (OSINT) research collective based in the United Kingdom. CNEI functions as a platform for secondary school students and young analysts to develop analytical rigor, geospatial verification skills, and empirical research frameworks.

Analytical Standards & Non-Partisanship

Empirical Objectivity: CNEI outputs are strictly factual. Personal political opinions, partisan bias, and ideological stances are explicitly barred from influencing research outputs.

Anti-Corruption & Integrity: CNEI members operate strictly on a voluntary basis and do not solicit, accept, or offer payments or favors intended to influence or alter research findings.

Interpersonal Conduct: CNEI maintains strict professional standards within all internal communication channels.

Compliance & OSINT Boundaries

Public Sources Only: All data gathered and published by CNEI is sourced exclusively from unclassified, publicly available, and lawfully accessible open sources.

Strict Prohibitions: CNEI members do not procure, handle, store, or distribute hacked material, leaked classified government documents, or non-consensual personal identifying information (PII).

Statutory Compliance: All research activities strictly comply with applicable UK legislation, including the Computer Misuse Act 1990, Data Protection Act 2018 / UK GDPR, and Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (CDPA).
