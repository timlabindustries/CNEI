Easy to Understand Research

We Help You Understand World News

CNEI is a group of friendly researchers. We look at public facts, maps, and pictures to explain what is happening around the world in very simple words.

Read Our Reports Learn About Us

What We Do

01 // FIND FACTS

Public Pictures & Maps

We look at pictures taken from space and map tracking to see where planes and ships go.

02 // KEEP IT SIMPLE

Easy Explanations

We explain big world events so anyone can understand them without using confusing words.

03 // TRIPLE CHECK

Always Honest

We check our facts three times to make sure we only share true information.

Who We Are
CNEI is a free, independent group of students and researchers. We only use information that anyone can see on the internet. We do not work for the government or military.
