Institutional Profile

The Centre of National and External Intelligence (CNEI) operates as an independent, un-incorporated research collective providing open-source intelligence (OSINT) and strategic analysis on national security and geostrategic affairs.

Our methodology relies strictly on lawful, open-source verification pipelines including satellite imagery, flight telemetry, and maritime tracking data to deliver unbiased risk assessments.
