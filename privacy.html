Statutory Disclosure

Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: 11 SEPTEMBER 2026 | ENTITY: CNEI

1. Scope & Status

The Centre of National and External Intelligence (CNEI) operates as an unincorporated, voluntary open-source intelligence (OSINT) research collective under the laws of England and Wales. This Privacy Policy details our technical compliance under the Data Protection Act 2018 and the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR).

2. Information Architecture & Data Minimisation

In accordance with the principle of Data Minimisation (Article 5(1)(c) UK GDPR), this website operates purely as a static informational platform.

  • No Direct Data Collection: We do not host public registration systems, user accounts, interactive comment sections, or web-based contact forms.
  • Direct Email Communications: When you contact us via any official @cnei.org.uk email address (including legal@cnei.org.uk or info@cnei.org.uk), we process your email address, name, and message contents strictly to address your enquiry. The lawful basis for this processing is Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR (Legitimate Interests).
  • Server Logs & Technical Infrastructure: Our static assets are served via GitHub Pages. Infrastructure-level connection logs (including IP addresses, user-agent strings, and request timestamps) are processed independently by GitHub Inc. as an external infrastructure provider to maintain network security and denial-of-service resilience.

3. Data Retention & Third-Party Disclosure

Personal data obtained via direct email correspondence sent to any @cnei.org.uk address is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purpose of the enquiry, up to a strict maximum of 12 months, after which it is permanently purged. CNEI does not sell, lease, trade, or transfer personal data to any commercial entities or third parties.

4. Your Statutory Rights

Under Chapter III of the UK GDPR, you maintain explicit rights regarding any personal data we hold about you, including the right of access, rectification, erasure, and restriction of processing. Enquiries or Data Subject Access Requests (DSARs) must be directed to any official @cnei.org.uk address (such as legal@cnei.org.uk). You reserve the right to lodge a formal complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Information Provenance & Operational Notice All analytical outputs and materials published by CNEI are derived strictly from publicly available, unclassified, and lawfully accessible open sources. CNEI does not handle, store, or process classified government documents, leaked materials, or non-consensual personal data. CNEI is an independent, unincorporated, voluntary research collective operated by student researchers across the United Kingdom and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government body, military authority, or official intelligence agency.
