Analytical Standards

Research Methodology

The rigorous verification framework guiding every report published by CNEI. Real insight is built on accuracy, patience, and doing the work properly.

PHASE 01 // DATA INGESTION

Unclassified & Public Ingestion

CNEI strictly ingests data from publicly available, unclassified, and lawfully accessible open sources. Our feeds include commercial satellite imagery, open Automatic Identification System (AIS) maritime telemetry, ADS-B flight tracking data, public regulatory filings, and open academic literature. We do not handle, request, or process classified or leaked documents.

PHASE 02 // VERIFICATION

Spatial & Telemetry Cross-Referencing

No single feed is taken as truth. Every datapoint undergoes multi-angle cross-verification. Satellite imagery is matched against ground coordinates, environmental conditions, and timestamped telemetry. We verify spatial evidence line by line and image by image to confirm physical consistency before drawing analytical conclusions.

PHASE 03 // AUDIT

Bias Scrubbing & Noise Reduction

Analytical outputs must be free from speculation, sensation, and editorial bias. Our analysts strip away narrative framing, social media hype, and unverified commentary, filtering data until only raw, demonstrable facts remain. If a data gap exists, it is explicitly declared rather than filled with guesswork.

PHASE 04 // PUBLICATION

Transparent Output & Provenance

Every published report includes clear methodology references, source citations, and confidence markers. By making our analytical pipeline transparent, readers can independently verify the underlying data and audit our findings line by line.

Information Provenance & Operational Notice All analytical outputs and materials published by CNEI are derived strictly from publicly available, unclassified, and lawfully accessible open sources. CNEI does not handle, store, or process classified government documents, leaked materials, or non-consensual personal data. CNEI is an independent, unincorporated, voluntary research collective operated by student researchers across the United Kingdom and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government body, military authority, or official intelligence agency.
