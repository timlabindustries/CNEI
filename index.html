Information Provenance & Operational Notice All analytical outputs published by CNEI are derived exclusively from publicly accessible, lawful open sources. CNEI does not handle or process classified government documents or non-consensual personal data. CNEI is an independent, un-incorporated voluntary student-led research collective and is not affiliated with any official government body or intelligence agency.
