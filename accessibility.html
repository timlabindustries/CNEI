Accessibility Statement

EFFECTIVE DATE: 11 SEPTEMBER 2026 | ENTITY: CNEI

1. Operational Commitment

CNEI is dedicated to providing digital accessibility across all platforms for individuals with disabilities, screen-reader users, and Welsh-language speakers across Wales. We target compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards.

2. Structural & Technical Design

To ensure lightweight, accessible navigation:

  • The site uses clean HTML5 structural tags and semantic headings.
  • External web-font dependencies and script-heavy layout shifts have been eliminated to ensure clear contrast and rapid rendering on assistive devices.

3. Welsh Language (Cymraeg) Policy

In recognition of our operations within Wales:

  • An integrated, zero-tracking translation interface is built directly into cnei.org.uk to render the entire web portal accessible in Welsh (Cymraeg).
  • We accept, welcome, and process official enquiries and accessibility requests in Welsh. Correspondence sent in Welsh to any official @cnei.org.uk email address receives equal response priority without delay.

4. Remediation & Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or require content in an alternative structure, contact our team:

Email: Any designated @cnei.org.uk email address (e.g., info@cnei.org.uk or legal@cnei.org.uk).
Response SLA: We aim to acknowledge and address accessibility enquiries within 5 working days.

Information Provenance & Operational Notice All analytical outputs and materials published by CNEI are derived strictly from publicly available, unclassified, and lawfully accessible open sources. CNEI does not handle, store, or process classified government documents, leaked materials, or non-consensual personal data.
