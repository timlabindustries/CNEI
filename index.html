Core Operational Pillars

Standard 01

Empirical Analysis

Grounded strictly in verifiable, publicly accessible data. Free from speculation, ideological bias, or partisan narrative.

Standard 02

Unclassified & Lawful

Operating strictly within UK legal frameworks (Computer Misuse Act 1990, DPA 2018) and platform terms of service.

Standard 03

Independent Research

Operated autonomously by student researchers committed to academic standards, open-source verification, and transparent methodology.
