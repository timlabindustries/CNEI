Coalition of Nuclear Energy Initiatives

The Coalition of Nuclear Energy Initiatives (CNEI) is an independent research and advocacy collective dedicated to promoting a pragmatic, science-first approach to clean energy and grid security.

We established CNEI because we recognized that the path to a sustainable future requires confronting the physical realities of grid operation, energy density, and baseload security head-on. Our goal is to cut through political posturing and oversimplified narratives, examining the empirical data to provide a clear-eyed perspective on what it actually takes to power a modern economy.

Core Principles

We believe in clean, sustainable, and reliable power.

For too long, the public debate has forced a false choice: treating intermittent renewables and nuclear energy as competing options rather than essential partners. While wind and solar play a vital role in our energy mix, treating them as a standalone solution for an industrial society ignores the fundamental challenge of grid intermittency.

We advocate for an engineering-first strategy that places advanced nuclear energy at the heart of our net-zero goals. Nuclear power provides high-density, weather-independent generation capable of supporting millions of homes and heavy industries continuously, without relying on fossil fuel backups during periods of low renewable output.

The Case for Nuclear Energy

When examining the technical data, the advantages of a robust nuclear baseload are clear:

  • Unrivaled Reliability: Nuclear facilities operate with an exceptionally high capacity factor, delivering continuous baseload electricity more than 90% of the time. This operational consistency stands in stark contrast to weather-dependent sources, which fluctuate based on environmental conditions rather than consumer demand.
  • Grid Stability and Energy Security: Relying entirely on intermittent sources requires massive, expensive grid storage or fossil-fuel backup plants to prevent blackouts. Nuclear energy bypasses this vulnerability, securing national independence and steady power generation regardless of seasonal shifts or international fuel price shocks.
  • Long-Term Infrastructure and Economic Growth: Modern nuclear plants and emerging Small Modular Reactor (SMR) designs offer operational lifespans exceeding 60 years. This investment brings thousands of high-skilled engineering, technical, and operational jobs to local economies, establishing a lasting industrial foundation.

Our Mission

We are committed to providing objective analysis and transparent education on modern energy policy. We want a future where energy security is guaranteed, consumer costs are insulated from volatile fossil fuel markets, and climate action aligns strictly with the laws of science and engineering.
