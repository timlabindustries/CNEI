About CNEI

Institutional Structure & Mission

An un-incorporated, voluntary research collective delivering rigorous, independent open-source intelligence (OSINT) on national security and geostrategic affairs.

Institutional Status

The Center for National & External Intelligence (CNEI) operates as a voluntary, un-incorporated research collective composed of students and independent researchers across the United Kingdom. We are not a government body, military authority, or official state intelligence agency.

Data Ingestion & Provenance

All analyses produced by CNEI rely exclusively on publicly available, legally accessible open-source data (OSINT). CNEI does not solicit, receive, or process classified documents, leaked material, or non-consensual personal data.

Analytical Standards

Our primary objective is the elimination of speculative reporting and partisan bias. Every publication adheres to a multi-tiered verification framework to guarantee analytical integrity, precision, and complete transparent citations.

Information Provenance & Operational Notice All analytical outputs published by CNEI are derived exclusively from publicly accessible, lawful open sources. CNEI does not handle or process classified government documents or non-consensual personal data. CNEI is an independent, un-incorporated voluntary student-led research collective and is not affiliated with any official government body or intelligence agency.
