About the Centre

Origin & Evolution

The Centre of National and External Intelligence (CNEI) started with a shared obsession: understanding the world through the noise. There was no grand institution behind it, no corporate backing, and no shortcuts. Just a few researchers, endless feeds of open data, and the discipline to sift through facts line by line, image by image.

We built CNEI from the ground up: learning the methodologies, testing the sources, cross-referencing telemetry, and verifying spatial data long into the night. We learned early on that real insight isn't found in hype or guesswork—it’s built on accuracy, patience, and doing the work properly.

Today, that same ground-level discipline guides every report we publish. We collect only what is unclassified and public, scrutinise every source, and strip away bias until only raw, verifiable evidence remains.

Collective Structure & Governance

CNEI operates as an independent, unincorporated, and voluntary research collective run by student researchers across the United Kingdom. Operating outside traditional political or corporate pressures enables our analysts to maintain total objectivity across all research areas.

ENTITY STATUS: Unincorporated Voluntary Research Collective
RESEARCH BASE: United Kingdom (Student Researchers)
DATA CLASS: 100% Unclassified / Publicly Accessible Open Source
AFFILIATION: None (Independent of Government & Military Bodies)
Information Provenance & Operational Notice All analytical outputs and materials published by CNEI are derived strictly from publicly available, unclassified, and lawfully accessible open sources. CNEI does not handle, store, or process classified government documents, leaked materials, or non-consensual personal data. CNEI is an independent, unincorporated, voluntary research collective operated by student researchers across the United Kingdom and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government body, military authority, or official intelligence agency.
